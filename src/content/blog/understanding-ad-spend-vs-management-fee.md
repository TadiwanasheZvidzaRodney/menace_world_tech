## Two costs, two different jobs

An advertising proposal can be hard to evaluate when the amount for the agency's work and the amount for the advertising platform are bundled together. They are separate costs, and you should be able to see what each one covers before you agree to a campaign.

## What Is Ad Spend?

Ad spend is the budget used by the advertising platform, such as Meta, Google, or TikTok. Agree an amount before a campaign starts, and make sure you understand how it will be funded and reported. Your business should retain control of its advertising account and payment arrangement.

Audience, offer, timing, creative, and the customer journey all influence views, clicks, enquiries, and sales. Agree what to measure before launch, then use campaign data to refine your approach.

## What Is a Management Fee?

A management fee pays for the agreed work that supports a campaign. Depending on the scope, this may include:

- **Strategy:** Deciding who to reach, what to say, and when to say it.
- **Creative direction and copy:** Writing the ad and creating its visuals or video.
- **Campaign setup:** Setting up the campaign and its audiences.
- **Monitoring and refinement:** Reviewing activity and making agreed adjustments.
- **Reporting:** Explaining the numbers and suggesting what to do next.

Confirm what is included, how often work will be reviewed, what access is needed, and how the work will be reported. A clear scope helps you understand what the fee pays for.

## A simple budget illustration

For illustration only, imagine a business has $300 available for a month and agrees to allocate $200 to campaign management and $100 to platform spend. The split shows how the two costs can be planned separately; it does not predict reach or sales.

Your actual allocation should reflect your business priorities, campaign scope, and available budget. Ask for the two amounts and the services they cover in writing.
